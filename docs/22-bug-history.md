## Severity Assessment

No defect in this history reached production with user-facing data loss, a security breach, or a cost-safety violation. The two closest-to-serious findings were both caught *before* being relied upon: the missing `StructuredOutputParser`/`LeakageGuard` milestones (entry 5) were caught during development, before either had a real caller in production traffic, and the OpenRouter leak (entry 2) was caught by the conformance harness specifically designed to catch it before any model was shipped as a validated default. This track record is presented as evidence for, not a substitute for, continued conformance validation of any future provider/model addition.
